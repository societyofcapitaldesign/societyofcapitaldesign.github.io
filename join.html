How to Join

Join a chapter

Speak to the SCD chapter lead at your school. They handle sign ups, distribute the Bulletin, and coordinate submissions for each essay cycle. Or join us directly through SLACK

Start a new chapter

Email the founders with your school name and your name. We provide everything: the competition materials, the Bulletin, the rubric, and the network.
