For club presidents

Join the Network

What your club keeps. Your name, your identity, your leadership, and full control of what you run at your school. Affiliation adds, it does not absorb.

What your members gain. Entry to every SCD competition cycle, the Bulletin every issue, invitations to network events, and their work published alongside students from other schools and countries.

What your chapter lead gains. A named position in a cross school network, judging and marking experience against a published rubric, and a track record that is verifiable because we only publish what happened.

How to join. Email us with your club name, school, and a short description of what you currently run. We reply to every message.
